Privacy Policy

Introduction

Checked Picks ("we", "our", "the site") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

Information you provide. We do not require an account to read this site. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to share in that message.

Automatically collected information. Like most websites, this site automatically receives certain information from your browser, including your IP address (truncated for analytics purposes), browser type, device type, the pages you view, the referring URL, and the date/time of access.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Analytics — Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used (page views, scroll depth, click behavior).
  • Advertising measurement — Microsoft (Bing Ads UET) and Meta (Facebook Pixel) to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.
  • Affiliate tracking — Outbound links to merchant partners (such as BuyGoods) include parameters that allow the merchant to attribute your purchase to this site.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and opt out of Google Analytics using the official Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Third-Party Services

When you click an outbound link on this site, you leave Checked Picks and enter the destination site (for example, the official CogniCare Pro page operated by the manufacturer or by BuyGoods). The destination site is governed by its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the site, measure traffic and engagement, evaluate advertising performance, respond to inquiries, and comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share aggregated, non-identifying data with the analytics and advertising vendors listed above. We may disclose information when required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain uses. Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA) and the European Economic Area (GDPR) have specific rights under their local laws. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@checkedpicks.com.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version.
