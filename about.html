About Checked Picks

Who We Are

Checked Picks is an independent affiliate publisher focused on cognitive wellness, brain fog, memory support, and related natural supplement categories. We are not a manufacturer, retailer, or healthcare provider. Our purpose is to put together clear, plain-English buyer's guides that help adult readers compare options and make informed purchase decisions.

How We Make Money

This site participates in affiliate programs operated by retailers such as BuyGoods. When a reader clicks an outbound link on this site and completes a qualifying purchase on the merchant's official page, we may earn a commission — at no additional cost to the reader. Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial process or which products we choose to cover.

Editorial Approach

We review what is publicly published by the manufacturer, the official supplement facts panel, and publicly available research on the listed ingredients. We do not run our own clinical trials, we do not claim to diagnose or treat any condition, and we do not present individual testimonials as medical evidence.

Where we cite research, we link to the source. Where information is the manufacturer's claim, we say so. Where a question has no clear answer in publicly available material, we say that too.

For corrections, source requests, or partnership inquiries, write to contact@checkedpicks.com.
